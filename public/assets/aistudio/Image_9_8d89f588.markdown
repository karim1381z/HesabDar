## Brand & Style

This design system translates the luminous geometry of Persian *Orosi* (traditional stained-glass lattice architecture) into a contemporary, sovereign wealth fintech experience. It merges high-density jewel tones with disciplined structural framing. The emotional tone conveys ancestral permanence, mathematical precision, and bespoke private banking prestige. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Luminous Glassmorphic Depth:** Semi-translucent colored panels inspired by sunlight streaming through pigmented glass, supported by crisp internal specular highlights.
- **Architectural Continuity:** Eradication of generic floating white cards. Layouts are engineered as contiguous stages: anchored header stages flow seamlessly into grounded sheets, nested compartments, and interconnected ledger tracks.
- **Crystalline Contrast:** Deep nocturnal substrates juxtaposed against vivid lapis, turquoise, saffron, amethyst, and ruby accents, balanced with razor-thin hairline borders that echo wooden sash latticework (*Sasheh*).

## Colors

The color palette is derived entirely from mineral pigments and architectural stained-glass palettes:

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Persian Lapis):** Used for structural stage anchoring, primary interactive foundations, and deep velvet floodfills.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Nishapur Turquoise):** The color of verification, active financial flows, liquidity metrics, and positive movement.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Warm Saffron Amber Gold):** Reserved for tier badges, sovereign wealth indicators, VIP yield callouts, and critical focal points.
- **Accent Amethyst (`#6D28D9` - Royal Amethyst):** Utilized for secondary account partitions, asset yield cards, and security credentials.
- **Accent Ruby (`#BE185D` - Ruby Rose):** Used for critical alerts, spending outlays, high-priority actions, and security prompts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#09090B` - Obsidian Pitch):** The absolute dark canvas substrate beneath the translucent sheets, ensuring total contrast and optical clarity.

Surfaces do not use pure white. All positive contrast text and light surfaces utilize **Alabaster Silk (`#F8FAFC`)** or tinted parchment whites at varying opacities (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.04)` to `0.85`).

## Typography

Typography pairs rigorous geometric structures with deliberate financial legibility. The primary structural Latin font maps directly into the Space Grotesk family, tailored to harmonize with Vazirmatn when rendering bilingual Farsi/English banking interfaces.

- **Weight Disciplines:** Use strictly 500 (Medium) for body copy and dense account metadata; 700 (Bold) for list items, card metrics, and structural titles; 800 (ExtraBold) for sectional stages; and 900 (Black) for sovereign balances, large currency representations, and primary KPI hero headers.
- **Numbers & Currencies:** Tabular figures must be enabled by default (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) for currency amounts, IBANs, and percentage metrics to maintain vertical alignment within financial ledgers.
- **Icons:** All visual symbols rely solely on Material Symbols Outlined, configured with a 2px stroke, optical size of 24, and sharp corner terminations to echo geometric sash detailing.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine rejects standard fragmented cards in favor of a **Continuous Stage & Sheet Architecture**:

- **Continuous Stage Header:** The viewport begins with an uncontained, deeply saturated Persian Lapis canvas hosting the user profile, aggregate net wealth, and rapid liquidity triggers.
- **Connected Frosted Sheet:** Emerging directly underneath the stage header, the primary interactive content sheet docks into view with an uninterrupted top border radius of 32px (`2rem`). This sheet features backdrop blurring (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) and extends continuously to the lower viewport boundaries.
- **Nested Lattice Hierarchy:** Within the sheet, modules are not floating islands; they are nested structural bays partitioned by continuous hairline timeline tracks (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Zero outer margin for the main sheet; content uses `margin: 1rem`. Stage headers take up 30-40% viewport height.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with pinned vertical timeline rails and side-docked vault selectors.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. The stage header morphs into a persistent left control column (4 columns), while the interactive frosted sheet spans the right 8 columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft gray drop shadows. Elevation is governed by translucent color stacking, internal refraction, and hair-thin lattice boundaries:

- **Stage Floor (Elevation 0):** Obsidian Pitch (`#09090B`) with deep radial atmospheric gradients of Persian Lapis (`#1E1B4B`) and Nishapur Turquoise (`#0D9488`).
- **Main Sheet (Elevation 1):** Tinted glass base (`rgba(15, 12, 41, 0.75)`) backed by a 24px backdrop filter blur. Top edge receives a continuous 1px specular light rim: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Nested Vault Containers (Elevation 2):** High-contrast jewel inserts (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.45)` or `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`), banded by 1px borders colored via `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Active Interactive Layer (Elevation 3):** Modal sheets and critical confirmations utilize elevated glass with an amber/saffron ambient bleed: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -8px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.25), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Timeline Dividers:** Vertical connector rails are expressed with 1px hairline paths featuring vertical linear gradient strokes: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.5) 0%, rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.2) 50%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) 100%)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances contemporary usability with the arched, sweeping forms of Persian Orosi architecture:

- **Main Architectural Sheets:** Anchor at the top edge with a monumental 32px (`2rem`) radius on top-left and top-right corners, while flat at the sides and base.
- **Nested Containers & Bays:** Default to a controlled `0.5rem` (8px) radius, preventing visual clutter when multiple modules sit contiguous to one another.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Utilize `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) pill silhouettes, providing immediate tactile affordance.
- **Framing Insets:** Decorative corners and structural chips utilize subtle 45-degree corner clips on non-interactive ledger tags to mimic faceted stained-glass framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Sovereign Gold / Amber):** Solid Warm Saffron Amber (`#B45309`) background with crisp typography in white (`#FFFFFF`), overlaid with an internal top specular border (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`). Active state applies a luminous amber bloom.
- **Secondary (Turquoise Glass):** Nishapur Turquoise tint (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.18)`), 1px Nishapur border (`#0D9488`), text color `#2DD4BF`.
- **Tertiary (Lattice Ghost):** Transparent fill, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text color `#E2E8F0`. Hover triggers background fill of `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.5)`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Compact height (32px), pill-shaped with 1px hairline border. Unselected states sit at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill with muted typography. Active selections ignite with Royal Amethyst (`#6D28D9`) or Turquoise (`#0D9488`) fills and high-contrast white text.

### Nested Ledger Lists & Timeline Dividers
- Lists do not use isolated bubble cards. Individual transactions share a continuous grounded tray.
- Items are divided by internal hairlines. Every item links to a continuous left-aligned timeline spine (`2px` thick) with node markers denoting transaction categories (e.g., Turquoise node for deposits, Ruby Rose for debits, Saffron for investments).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Geometric square (checkbox) and octagonal-inspired round (radio) profiles with 1.5px borders.
- Checked state fills with Persian Lapis and Nishapur Turquoise check icons, illuminated by an external micro-glow matching the accent tone.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural slots. Fill is dark obsidian glass (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`), bordered with a 1px muted frame (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- On focus: Border transitions to Nishapur Turquoise (`#0D9488`) with a matching `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.2)` glow ring. Labels remain pinned in 700 weight, uppercase, tracking `0.04em`.

### Vault Containers (Card Alternatives)
- Seamless connected panels with dark jewel fills (blended Lapis, Amethyst, and Turquoise). 
- Specular top rim (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). High contrast typography guarantees primary balance values are immediately scannable against colored backdrops.
